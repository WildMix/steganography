    This follows EMBEDDING_WALKTHROUGH.md: exactly HELLO, keyed mode, balanced, and auto. The four-pixel equations are the walkthrough’s deliberately small teaching example, not a slice of the real 1,024-state trellis.

    The landscape is a generated 512 × 512 grayscale teaching image. Its encrypted bytes, change locations, cost map, balancing scores, and recovered message come from running this repository’s Python/C++ encoder. The demonstration uses the walkthrough’s public counting key, salt, and padding. Real embeddings use a secret key and fresh cryptographic randomness.

    This animation explains how the implementation works. A subtle-looking image and a lower balancing score do not prove that a detector cannot find the message. PNG must be preserved losslessly; resizing and JPEG conversion can destroy the payload. Authentication checks the extracted payload and context, not every image pixel.

    Everything runs locally with no dependencies or network requests. Reproduction and controls · Teaching cover · Verified stego PNG
