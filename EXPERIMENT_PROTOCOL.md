# Frozen evaluation protocol

The agreed provisional target is detector ROC-AUC no greater than 0.55. A conservative empirical pass requires the paired, source-group bootstrap 95% interval to stay within [0.45, 0.55] for each evaluated classical detector: equivalently, the upper bound considering either score orientation must be at most 0.55. This prevents a strongly reversed classifier from being mistaken for security. Score orientation and deployment thresholds are still chosen on validation only. Deep learning is reported separately and must not be silently omitted from the conclusion. Intervals are per detector, not a familywise or universal certificate.

The original 10,000 BOSSbase grayscale PGM rasters are retained without resizing or pixel preprocessing. PNG serialization is identical for covers and stegos. This is a processed photographic benchmark source, not evidence about every camera or ordinary publishing workflow.

Before any detector fitting, group exact pixel duplicates and approximate 64-bit difference hashes within four bits. Put every connected group in one partition. The fixed split targets are 1,200 training images, 400 validation images, 1,000 final test images, and the remainder as unused reserve. Actual counts may exceed targets at group boundaries. Camera/session metadata is unavailable, so unseen-camera generalization cannot be claimed. Hash grouping is an imperfect proxy for related scenes.

Develop candidates on training and validation only. Candidate gross rates are 0.05, 0.025, 0.01, and 0.005 bpp; candidate strategies are the original blueprint and experimental conditional residual sign balancing. Balance only modification signs; verify unchanged parity, modification locations, and authenticated recovery. No assertion of research priority or universal security is attached to this new combination.

Classical detectors are a 64-member shrinkage discriminant ensemble over randomized residual-feature subspaces and an independently trained extra-trees classifier. Features include fourth-order residual co-occurrences at multiple quantizations, activity-conditioned co-occurrences, intensity and parity statistics, RS-like regularity, and PNG compressed length. These are explicitly a custom rich-feature set, not the complete published SRM implementation. Before the first detector fit, two standalone diagnostics were also specified: adjacent-intensity-bin chi-square imbalance and RS-like regular/singular group imbalance. They use validation-selected score orientation and thresholds, but no fitted feature transformation; the latter is not presented as a calibrated classic RS payload estimator.

The deep detector is a compact residual CNN with fixed high-pass preprocessing, an unpooled early residual block, later residual blocks, and mean/second-moment aggregation. Train with paired cover/stego 128-pixel crops and geometric augmentation. Validation and testing average four disjoint 256-pixel quadrants to cover all image pixels. This is not a reproduction of full SRNet, and CPU-scale training limits its strength.

Train an intentionally detectable control containing random LSB replacement at 0.4 bpp. Its success confirms basic detector operation, not sensitivity to every low-rate adaptive algorithm. Before any fit results are available, set a control sensitivity gate of validation AUC lower confidence bound greater than 0.75 for the residual-feature and neural detectors; weak standalone statistics are reported without treating their chance results as strong evidence. Select score direction, decision threshold, and deep checkpoint on validation only. Freeze models before loading final-test features. Report actual FPR alongside detection at a threshold calibrated to 1% FPR on validation.

Generate fresh per-image experiment salts from a documented deterministic stream, using a fixed public benchmark key to expose possible reused-key artifacts. Production uses operating-system randomness. Every generated candidate undergoes actual embedding, lossless PNG serialization, and authenticated extraction. A modification simulator is not substituted for the encoder.

Keep paired cover/stego predictions together and bootstrap near-duplicate groups, not individual labels. Intervals are conditional on the fitted model and sampled source; they do not include uncertainty over all possible detector training procedures. Report admission failures without silently excluding images.

Only after selecting the most capable passing validation candidate, generate its test population and the predeclared baseline comparator, then evaluate the frozen models once. If a final candidate fails and redesign continues, retire that test population as development evidence and use previously untouched reserve groups for confirmation. Preserve failed results and explain every revision.

Artifacts include the source checksum, fixed manifest, exact image identifiers, per-image modification and timing metrics, cached sample arrays, feature arrays, trained classical and neural models, validation history, final predictions, and JSON summaries. Reproducible commands and limitations belong in the final report.
